Before you get in touch

  • Check the FAQ and troubleshooting page. Most setup questions are answered there.
  • Make sure you're on the latest version of Plinth ({{ site.theme_version }}). Your version is shown in Online Store > Themes.
  • If something looks broken, try it on an unedited copy of the theme with apps turned off. That helps us tell a theme issue from an app or code conflict.

What happens next

  • You'll get an automatic email confirming we've received your request. If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder.
  • We reply from our support inbox to the email address you gave us.

How we handle your message

The contact form is handled by FormBackend, a form service. When you send it, your name, email address, store URL, theme version, topic, message and any attached files are sent to FormBackend, which stores them and emails them to our support inbox. FormBackend's handling of that data is covered by its privacy policy.

  • We use what you send only to answer your request. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your details with anyone else.
  • Submissions and attached files are deleted automatically from FormBackend after 90 days. Our replies stay in our support inbox.
  • To have your request deleted sooner, ask us in a reply or with a new request.
  • Never send passwords, staff account access or customer data. If we need store access, we'll ask for a collaborator request through Shopify instead.

Open a support request on GitHub

You can also open a support request on GitHub (a free GitHub account is needed), or browse existing requests to see if your question has already been answered. GitHub requests are public, so leave out anything private.

What support covers

  • Covered: installing and setting up Plinth, using its settings and sections, and fixing problems with the theme's own features.
  • Not covered: custom code changes, third-party apps, and Shopify settings that aren't part of the theme. We'll still help you work out whether a problem comes from the theme. For store and account questions, contact Shopify Help Center.
