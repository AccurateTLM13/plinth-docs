## What's included

- **A flagship-first home page:** hero, featured product band, feature grid, add-ons grid, comparison table and guarantee band. Every section can be reordered, hidden or removed.
- **A rich product page:** a media gallery with video and 3D, color swatches, custom text and dropdown options, subscriptions, gift card recipient details, store pickup, a sticky add-to-cart bar and related or complementary products.
- **A slide-out cart drawer** with add-on suggestions, discounts and an optional order note.
- **Collection filters and search suggestions** powered by Shopify Search & Discovery.
- **Country, currency and language selectors** for selling internationally.
- **Built for accessibility and speed:** keyboard and screen reader support throughout, and no heavy scripts.
