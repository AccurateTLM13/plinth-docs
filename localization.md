## Countries and currencies

1. Set up the countries you sell to in **Settings > Markets**. Each market can have its own currency.
2. Plinth's **country/region selector** lets shoppers pick their country. Prices then show in that country's currency.

The selector shows the country name with its currency, for example "Canada (CAD $)". Shoppers press **Update** to apply a change, so the page never jumps unexpectedly.

## Languages

1. Add and publish languages in **Settings > Languages**.
2. Plinth's **language selector** lists each language in its own name, for example "Français" and "Deutsch".
3. Translate your content (products, collections, pages and theme text) with the free **Translate & Adapt** app, or a translation app of your choice.

## Where the selectors appear

| Location | Setting | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Footer | **Footer** section > **Show country/region selector** and **Show language selector** | On |
| Header | **Header** section > **Show country/region selector** and **Show language selector** | Off |

On phones, header selectors move into the menu drawer.

A selector only appears when there's a choice to make: the country selector needs more than one country in your markets, and the language selector needs more than one published language. If you turn one on and it doesn't appear, the theme editor shows a hint explaining why.

## Changing theme text

All the text built into Plinth (button labels, messages, headings like "Your cart") can be changed without code:

1. Go to **Online Store > Themes**.
2. Next to Plinth, select **⋯ > Edit default theme content**.
3. Search for the text you want to change, and enter your own wording.

Plinth ships with English text. Add other languages through **Settings > Languages** and translate them with Translate & Adapt.
